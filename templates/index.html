How AASHA Works

Overcoming paperwork delays and connecting rural communities to clinical care.

1. VOICE

Speak naturally in local languages (Hindi, Hinglish, English). Zero cumbersome form typing during busy field rounds.

2. AI

Transcribes spoken notes, extracts symptoms, duration, observations, pregnancy notes, and formats clean structured summaries.

3. TRIAGE

Rule-based clinical engine flags warning signs into Urgent, Moderate, or Routine tiers, surfacing high-risk emergencies immediately.

4. CARE

Dedicated Primary Health Centre doctor receives prioritized cases and transmits actionable medical guidance back to the worker.

Clinical Safety & Ethical AI Guardrail

AASHA is strictly designed to assist healthcare workers with documentation and priority triage routing. The AI does not autonomously diagnose diseases or prescribe medications. Emergency clinical decisions and treatment plans remain under the direct supervision of qualified Medical Officers.
